# Character Backstories - Code of Meridaeia

## 🪓 Grom the Barbarian (Java Developer)
**Title**: The Outcast Warrior  
**Race**: Barbarian (Outcast)

### Backstory
Grom was born into the proud Barbarian clans of the Northern Wastes, where strength and honor were everything. But Grom was different - he questioned the old ways, sought knowledge over brute force, and believed in **optimization** rather than raw power.

His clan called him weak. They mocked his interest in "the ancient syntax" - the programming languages of the old world. When Grom suggested using **Object-Oriented principles** to organize their battle formations, the elders banished him.

Cast out and alone, Grom wandered until he found Valerion. There, he discovered his true calling: combining the warrior's discipline with the coder's precision. He became the **Java Barbarian** - a fighter who believes every problem has an elegant solution.

**His Quest**: Prove that intelligence and strength are not opposites. Restore the Great Compiler and show his clan they were wrong.

## 🧙 Malloc the Wizard (C++ Developer)
**Title**: The Memory Mage  
**Race**: Human Wizard

### Backstory
Malloc was once a student at the prestigious Arcane Academy, where the greatest mages studied the art of **memory manipulation**. But Malloc was small, awkward, and slow to cast spells. His peers bullied him relentlessly.

"You'll never master pointers," they sneered.  
"You'll cause memory leaks," they laughed.  
"You're too weak for C++," they said.

One day, during a particularly cruel prank, Malloc was locked in the Academy's forbidden library. There, he discovered ancient tomes about **manual memory management** - the lost art of malloc() and free(). While his peers relied on automatic garbage collection, Malloc learned to control memory directly.

He emerged from that library transformed. No longer the bullied student, but **Malloc the Memory Mage** - a wizard who could allocate and deallocate reality itself.

**His Quest**: Master the corrupted code of Meridaeia and prove that true power comes from understanding, not arrogance.

## 🛡️ Ser Handshake (Networking Paladin)
**Title**: The Broken Knight  
**Race**: Human Paladin

### Backstory
Ser Handshake was once the happiest man in Valerion. He had a loving wife, Elena, and a promising career as a royal messenger. His job was simple: establish **three-way handshakes** (SYN, SYN-ACK, ACK) to ensure reliable communication across the kingdom.

One fateful day, Elena was traveling to visit her family when bandits attacked the caravan. Ser Handshake sent the distress signal (SYN), but the response never came. The connection timed out. By the time he arrived, it was too late.

Consumed by guilt, he blamed himself. "If only I had used UDP for speed instead of TCP for reliability..." But he knew the truth - sometimes, even perfect protocols fail.

He became a Paladin, swearing an oath: **"I will never let another connection fail. I will protect every packet, every handshake, every transmission."**

**His Quest**: Restore the corrupted network of Meridaeia and ensure no one else loses someone due to a failed connection.

## 🏹 Artemis the Stream-Caller (Data Engineering Archer)
**Title**: The Transformed Twin  
**Race**: Dragonoid-Human (Formerly Elemari Akeyta)  
**Original Name**: Elemari Akeyta

### Backstory
Artemis and her twin brother Vulkun (Eke Voremikgadet) were born into the **Dragonoid-Human** race - a proud, god-like people who lived far from civilization in the volcanic highlands. They acted as deities in their domain, prideful and isolated.

But Elemari saw through the arrogance. She hated their people's folly, their refusal to help others, their obsession with treasure and power. She wanted to leave, to explore, to **stream** knowledge to the world instead of hoarding it.

One day, while searching for a secret tome near the Volcanoes of Valerion, they were ambushed by goblins and wolves. Just as they were about to win, the goblins summoned a **dark entity** - condensing the wolves into an abominable spectre.

The ghost terrified Eke, and he fell into the volcano's mouth.

Enraged, Elemari felt an arrow pierce her chest. She erupted into a **bright ball of purple light** - transforming into her true form. As **Artemis the Stream-Caller**, she rained arrows upon the goblins and eradicated the spectre.

Clutching her brother's torn cloth, she recited an old dragon's prayer. Suddenly, a humongous figure leapt from the volcano - **Vulkun**, revived but corrupted. His eyes glowed red, his memory fragmented.

**"Serath mat'han ora!"** (Sister, don't leave me!) he roared, mistaking her for an enemy. He hurled magma at her.

Before Artemis could change back, the magic consumed her, transporting her to **another dimension**. She left behind only her brother's cloth.

**Her Quest**: Master the data streams of Meridaeia, find a way back to her dimension, and reunite with her lost twin brother.

## 🐉 Vulkun of Ring Zero (Kernel Developer)
**Title**: The Corrupted Dragonoid  
**Race**: Dragonoid-Human (Corrupted)  
**Original Name**: Eke Voremikgadet

### Backstory
Vulkun doesn't remember his past clearly. Fragments flash through his mind:
- A sister's voice
- Falling into fire
- A prayer in an ancient tongue
- **"Serath mat'han ora!"** - words he doesn't understand

What he knows: He woke up in the heart of a volcano, his body transformed into pure **draconic power**. The magma didn't burn him - it **became** him. He had access to **Ring Zero** - the deepest level of reality's kernel.

But the transformation came at a cost. His memories are corrupted. His emotions are volatile. He can manipulate the very **kernel of existence**, but he doesn't know why he has this power or what he's supposed to do with it.

Sometimes, in his dreams, he sees a figure in purple light. An archer. A sister?

He searches for answers in the depths of Meridaeia, hoping the Great Compiler holds the key to his lost memories.

**His Quest**: Descend to Ring Zero of the Great Compiler, restore his corrupted memories, and discover the truth about his sister.
